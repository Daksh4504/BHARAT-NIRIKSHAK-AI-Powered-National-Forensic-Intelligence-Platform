Pillar 1 Focus

Disaster Victim Identification (DVI) Intelligence

Rapid, humane, and Interpol-standard biometric victim reconciliation for mass casualty incidents

🌐 Interpol DVI Scientific Concordance
Adheres strictly to Interpol Disaster Victim Identification guidelines, categorizing biometric data into Primary and Secondary identifiers to eliminate misidentifications:
Primary Identifiers
• Forensic Odontology: FDI two-digit dental notation charting.
• DNA STR Profiles: Locus match & allele concordance.
• Dactyloscopy: Friction ridge fingerprint patterns.
Secondary Identifiers
• Surgical Implants: Orthopedic plate/screw serial numbers.
• Dermal Markers: Scars, medical incisions, tattoos.
• Personal Property: Jewelry, clothing, inscribed items.
📜 Automated Reconciliation Certificate
Once concordance exceeds verified scientific thresholds, the system generates an official Interpol-format reconciliation certificate with complete ante-mortem medical corroboration and Section 174 CrPC inquest sign-offs.

Pillar 2 Focus

AI Crime Scene Evidence Prioritization

ISO/IEC 17025 scientific triage engine preventing degradation and accelerating FSL dispatch

Pillar A: Degradation Risk
Evaluates biochemical decay dynamics: temperature sensitivity, bacterial exposure, and biological half-life. High-risk items (e.g. wet blood stains, trace serology) are prioritized for immediate cold-chain lab forwarding.
Biological Half-Life
Pillar B: Evidentiary Value
Calculates direct probative linkage to the crime locus, suspect, and victim. Weapon firearms, latent fingerprints on points of entry, and definitive biological deposits receive top evidentiary weight.
Probative Linkage
Pillar C: Statutory Clock
Tracks judicial custody remand deadlines (14/60/90-day statutory charge-sheet limits) under Section 167/293 CrPC and BNSS. Flags evidence whose delayed analysis risks statutory bail for the accused.
Remand Compliance
🎯 Ranked FSL Lab Queue
Automates laboratory queuing: displays exhibit priority (Critical, High, Medium, Low), SLA targets (e.g. ≤12h for Critical serology), and dedicated division routing (DNA, Ballistics, Cyber, Toxicology).
📋 Form 27 Evidentiary Forwarding Manifest
One-click generator for official Section 293 CrPC forwarding documents. Embeds packaging verification, intact seal hashes, handler credentials, and SHA-256 cryptographic chain of custody.

Rather than basic pin maps, BHARAT NIRIKSHAK applies spatial statistics. A Moran's I index of +0.42 (p < 0.001) mathematically proves non-random spatial clustering, allowing police to distinguish high-risk systemic crime hubs from random background incidents.
